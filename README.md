# Rizal Rahman — Playful Scrapbook Portfolio

A handcrafted, paper-craft & scrapbook editorial portfolio website for **Rizal Rahman** (Digital Marketer & Graphic Designer, Manager at Monochrome FX), redesigned with pixel-perfect fidelity to the playful indie paper aesthetic (inspired by Aya Amr's Behance showcase).

  8. **Scrapbook Journal Blog (`blog.html`)**:
     - Separate blog page crafted in the exact same scrapbook and paper-craft aesthetic.
     - Live search filter and category pills (Digital Marketing, AI & Creative Tech, Design & Branding, Motion & Video).
     - Cards with washi tape, reading times, tags, and cumulative view counters.
     - Interactive notebook reader modal displaying the complete essay with callout notes.
  9. **Admin Portal (`admin.html`)**:
     - Secure management portal (passcode: `1234` or `admin`, with 1-click quick access button).
     - Metric cards: Total Articles, Published Live, Saved Drafts, and Cumulative Reads.
     - Full post editor with HTML formatting helper buttons and cover artwork presets.
     - Manage table: Edit, toggle published/draft status, delete, and restore default seed articles.

---

## ✨ Design Language & Aesthetic Features

- **Paper Canvas & Stationery Motifs**:
  - Warm sketchbook paper background texture (`#FAF8F5`).
  - Realistic **washi tape strips** with semi-translucent colors (butter yellow, rose pink, pastel blue, natural cream) pinning cards and notes at subtle angles.
  - Authentic **torn paper frames** and edges.
  - Realistic **ruled notebook paper** cards featuring binder hole punch rings down the left and red margin lines.
  - Grid/graph paper sheet centerpieces.

- **Playful Display Typography & Doodles**:
  - Chunky, bubbly marker cutout headers (`Fredoka`, `Titan One`) for big titles: `PORTFOLIO`, `Resume`, `Specialties`, `Selected Projects`.
  - Authentic handwriting (`Patrick Hand`, `Kalam`, `Caveat`) for notes, story paragraphs, and doodle labels.
  - Monospace typewriter metadata (`Space Mono`) for dates, contact details, and tech chips.
  - Pixel font badges (`Silkscreen`) for retro category tags.
  - Handcrafted SVG doodles:
    - Bold cobalt blue 8-point cutout star
    - Sunny yellow hand-drawn stars and blue outline doodle star
    - Red asterisk star push-pins
    - Playful black curly spiral squiggles
    - Creative tools doodle artwork: camera, magic drawing pencil with sparkle stars, film clapperboard, pixel cursor
    - Vintage telephone receiver handset with a looping curly dashed wire!

- **Sections & Architecture**:
