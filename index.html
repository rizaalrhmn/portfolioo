About Me ★

Hi! I'm Rizal, a multidisciplinary Digital Marketer and Graphic Designer currently serving as Manager at Monochrome FX in India.

My passion for creative work started with branding and visual identity—turning raw ideas into compelling narratives. Over the past 4+ years, I have helped brands scale through a blend of bold visual design, creative strategy, and performance-driven digital marketing.

Whether directing full-scale advertising campaigns, crafting generative AI imagery and cinematic video with Higgsfield AI, or designing packaging that stops people in their tracks, I believe great design always bridges human emotion with strategic business goals.

  • Role: Manager at Monochrome FX, India
  • Experience: 4+ years in Digital Marketing, Branding & Visual Identity
  • AI & Video: Generative AI imagery & cinematic video with Higgsfield AI
  • Design Focus: High-impact ad campaigns & standout packaging
